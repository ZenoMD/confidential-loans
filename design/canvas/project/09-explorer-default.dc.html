An outsider learns

Loan A was paid in full. Because loan B paid nothing, the public settled amount equals loan A's repayment. Loan B was impaired, then defaulted, and the Broker's public DebtTotal shows what the vault is still owed.

It doesn't learn

Either loan's principal, interest rate or periodic payment, loan B's amount owed, or either borrower's balance. If Borrower B made one payment before defaulting, the settled amount would mix both loans and hide loan A's repayment.
