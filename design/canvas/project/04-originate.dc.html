Public schedule

A confidential loan has no public interest rate or fees. Without reading the four encrypted terms, the ledger checks that the amount owed is at least the principal, that payments times the periodic payment cover the amount owed, and that the rate is at most 100%. The Broker builds the proof against the current Lending Balance, so loan B's proof is built after loan A validates.
